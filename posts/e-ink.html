E-ink Devices

October 21, 2026

There are many opinions on why people tend to use and not to use e-ink devices. However, there are a few reasons on why this could be a massive improvement if used correctly.

First off, e-ink devices have the name "e-ink" because of their screen which mimics a ink on a physical paper. They are very very slow because it has to move real, physical ink particles inside the screen to change the image.

And also they can be very expensive. Especially in Brazil, which a brand new Kindle Basic costs R$899 (which in terms of dollars is $164).

However, it is worth it?. Based on my experience yes, but only for reading.

If you don't want to spent money in many physical books (which most people like) and want a device to carry, i would say go for it.

People likely would use smartphones to read, but because of their screen which is mostly OLED or LCD, could make their eyes tired faster.

A e-ink display have a advantage over it, as it mimics a physical paper. Which helps to read for way more time over a smartphone.

There is a research about it from Harvard if you want to see: Paper Link

So which one to buy?

Well, it depends in your objective.

For a all-around size i would recomend a Kindle Basic/Paperwhite or a Kobo 6". Both of them are awesome for reading.

If you want a pocket-size reader go for a X-Teink X3 or a X4. I have both and the difference is in their size, as X3 is 3.7 inch and X4 4.3 inch.

The X-Teinks devices also are based in ESP32, which means that it runs on a microcontroller, which people make open-source firmwares, so it helps a lot of people to make their own tools without overstressing about it.

I heard that Boox also made a pocket-size version called Boox Pico. Also, this brand make smartphones and tablets with e-ink display.

So for the most reliable brands go for Kindle, Kobo, X-Teink, Bigme and BOOX.
